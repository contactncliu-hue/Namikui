Selected arrow color
The map always shows a fixed set of 6 teardrop markers plus 4 fixed landmark markers (2 Military Base stars, 2 Hospital crosses) and 1 house marker — adding or removing a group never adds or removes a teardrop marker; it only fills or empties one of the 6 teardrop slots (up to 10 groups total, the 7th–10th are "extra" and don't get their own teardrop, but their colors are still available for the arrow swatches below). The number badge on each group's tab (top-right of its block) cycles 1–10 and is just a label for that group — it does not change the map. Click a teardrop, star, cross, or house marker directly on the map to cycle its own number (1–10, then back to no number) — the marker's shape always stays the same and never disappears or changes; at the start of the cycle it simply shows no number. Click a teardrop marker to also select it for its own color — use the color picker above (same one used for a group's header color) to set that teardrop's color. Star, cross, and house markers keep their fixed shape and background colors and are not customisable, but their numbers are still clickable. Use the select boxes on the left to assign members (auto-pulls Faction & F1 Power from Members) · click a group's color swatch to recolor its table header (and its rally-path color options) · use a role's name dropdown to rename it — or pick "➕ Add New Role to List..." / "🗑 Remove a Role from List..." to manage the shared dropdown options so you don't have to keep retyping — the × deletes that row, and + ADD ROLE ROW adds a new one, all scoped to that one group, on the team you're currently viewing · click any small circle or arrow on the map to select it and rotate it (each click rotates it 25°; a full rotation returns it to a plain circle — the circle stays visible, it never disappears) — once selected, use the color picker above to set that one arrow's own color · use HIDE DEFAULT CIRCLES to temporarily hide any small circle markers that are still untouched (never rotated into an arrow and still the default color) so the map looks cleaner. Assigning a member here automatically sets their Slot (A or B, matching this tab) and Main/Substitute status on the most recent A/B Slots session. A member can only be assigned to one team (A or B) per session.
